# Journal of Conflict Resolution (journal-of-conflict-resolution)

## Journal positioning

JCR is the leading interdisciplinary journal on the causes, conduct, and resolution of conflict and cooperation, published by SAGE. Its centre of gravity is the quantitative and formal tradition of conflict research: large-N studies of interstate and civil war, terrorism, repression, alliances, bargaining, mediation and peacekeeping, game-theoretic models of crisis and cooperation, and experimental work on conflict behaviour. It reaches political scientists, economists, psychologists, and sociologists who share that toolkit, and a JCR paper is expected to state a theory of conflict or cooperation, derive expectations, and test them with data or models that a methodologically sophisticated cross-disciplinary reader will accept. Qualitative and historical work is not excluded, but it is rare and has to argue on the same terms.

This skill is a **fit / venue-selection / re-framing** tool. It does not replace the journal's current official submission guidelines. Before submitting, re-check the live author instructions on the SAGE site and the ScholarOne (Sage Track) submission system.

## When to trigger

- The author names JCR as the target venue, or wants the conflict-studies flagship with a quantitative/formal audience.
- An IR or comparative-politics paper is really about the dynamics of conflict, bargaining, or cooperation and needs re-framing for that community.
- A security or peace-research paper needs to be judged against JCR's theory-plus-test expectations and its inclusive word range.
- The author needs JCR's desk-reject risks and a credible alternative list (JPR, ISQ, IO, World Politics, BJPS, CPS, PSRM).

## Scope & topic fit

- Interstate conflict and crisis bargaining: deterrence, coercion, alliances, arms and technology, territorial disputes, escalation and termination.
- Civil war, insurgency, terrorism, repression, and post-conflict outcomes; rebel and state organisation; peacekeeping and peace agreements.
- Cooperation and its breakdown: negotiation, mediation, institutions, sanctions, trade and conflict, reputation and audience costs.
- Micro-foundations of conflict behaviour: experiments, surveys in conflict settings, leaders and psychology, public opinion and war.
- Formal and computational models of conflict, and methodological advances applied to conflict data (event data, networks, forecasting) with a substantive payoff.

## Method & evidence reporting boundary

- Report the active `empirical-research` core contract ID and revision and, as applicable, its registered estimand or theoretical target, assumptions, diagnostic dispositions, hash-bound Artifact IDs, and observed inference ceiling.
- Explain why those already-registered records matter to this venue's audience. This venue profile does not select estimators, clustering levels, thresholds, diagnostics, or repairs.
- If a required core record is absent or failed, return the design to `empirical-research` and report the resulting narrowed or withdrawn inference; do not repair it in the venue layer.
- Keep the venue-specific placement, data/code, disclosure, and transparency requirements below.

## Structure & house style

- Theory first: a JCR article states a mechanism about conflict or cooperation, derives observable expectations, and then tests them; a data-first paper with theory added afterwards reads as a fishing expedition to this audience.
- The introduction identifies the puzzle, the argument, the design, and the substantive stakes for understanding conflict; the literature is engaged across disciplines rather than within one IR school.
- The journal publishes Original Research Articles only, with an inclusive word range that has a minimum as well as a maximum, so short notes belong elsewhere.
- Chicago Manual of Style referencing; an abstract of no more than 150 words; at least four keywords; footnotes, references, tables, and graphs on separate pages in the submitted version.
- Full anonymisation for double-anonymised review, with all identifying material (acknowledgements, funding, ethics, data availability statement) on the title page rather than in the manuscript.
- Data sharing in a public repository and a data availability statement are conditions of publication; write the empirical sections so the deposited materials reproduce every reported exhibit.

## Verified submission facts (live-checked 2026-09-02; re-check before submitting)

- Publisher & submission system: SAGE; submissions via ScholarOne / Sage Track at https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/jcr (source: https://uk.sagepub.com/en-gb/eur/journal-of-conflict-resolution/journal200764).
- Review model: "Identity transparency: Double anonymized"; the journal "adheres to a rigorous double-anonymized reviewing policy" and requires two independent reviews for a Revise or Accept decision (source: SAGE-hosted copy of the journal's submission guidelines, https://www.sagepub.com/docs/default-source/msg/JCR, snapshot of https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/JCR dated 2025-06-25; the live page itself could not be fetched by automated tools on 2026-09-02).
- Word limit: "Total word count, including everything, should be at a minimum about 8,000 and not exceed 11,000 words", stated on the title page; abstract of no more than 150 words (source: https://www.sagepub.com/docs/default-source/msg/JCR, SAGE-hosted copy).
- Fees / APC policy for standard submissions: "There are no fees payable to submit or publish in this journal"; open access is optional via Sage Choice for a fee (source: https://www.sagepub.com/docs/default-source/msg/JCR, SAGE-hosted copy).
- Data / replication policy: "As a condition of publication authors are required to: Share your research data in a relevant public data repository" and include a data availability statement; if data cannot be shared, consult the editorial office (source: https://www.sagepub.com/docs/default-source/msg/JCR, SAGE-hosted copy).

## Pre-submission self-check

- [ ] One sentence stating the mechanism of conflict or cooperation this paper establishes and why a cross-disciplinary conflict scholar should care.
- [ ] The applicable core contract ID/revision, hash-bound diagnostics, and observed inference ceiling are reported without venue-layer alteration.
- [ ] The introduction positions the paper against the current conflict-research frontier across political science and neighbouring disciplines.
- [ ] Total word count sits inside the journal's minimum–maximum range and appears on the title page.
- [ ] Replication data and code are staged for a public repository and the data availability statement is drafted.

## Common desk-reject triggers

- Hiding, overriding, or rhetorically repairing a failed core diagnostic or observed inference ceiling instead of reporting the registered disposition.
- A descriptive or policy-oriented security paper with no theory of conflict and no test.
- A single-case historical narrative with no comparative or theoretical leverage, submitted to a quantitative/formal readership.
- A manuscript below the minimum or above the maximum of the inclusive word range, or one that is not fully anonymised.
- Out-of-scope topics (domestic politics or IR without a conflict/cooperation mechanism) that belong in a general journal.

## Re-routing decision

- Peace-research framing with a shorter format → Journal of Peace Research; broad IR theory and institutions → the `international-organization` deep pack; grand comparative-historical arguments → the `world-politics` deep pack.
- Comparative politics of civil conflict with a cross-national or subnational design → `comparative-political-studies`; general political-science significance → `british-journal-of-political-science` or the `apsr` / `ajps` deep packs.
- Method-first conflict-data contributions → `political-science-research-and-methods`; regional security politics → International Studies Quarterly or Security Studies.
